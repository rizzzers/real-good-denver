Weekly Denver Forecast

Denver opens the week soggy and a little grumpy about it, sliding from a mild 76 on Monday down to a damp, thunder flecked 65 by Wednesday as showers and storms move through each afternoon. The clouds clear out fast after that, with mostly sunny skies returning Thursday and a proper reward waiting: 75 on Friday, 78 on Saturday, and a calm 72 to close things out Sunday. Lows dip into the 40s and 50s all week, so hang onto the jacket even once the sun comes back.
