Denver Events

Book Swap - LINK
Monday, September 28, Fiction Beer Company, Free.
Bring two gently used books and leave with two new ones for your stack. It is a low key, no fuss way to refresh your reading pile over a pint at this Colfax Avenue taproom known for literary themed brews.

Teton Gravity Research: Actually, It Is Rocket Science - LINK
Friday, September 25, Boulder Theater, 27.28 to 38.61 dollars.
TGR's 31st annual ski and snowboard film tour hits the big screen with elite athletes and jaw dropping mountain cinematography. A beloved ritual for Colorado's outdoor obsessed crowd, best enjoyed with a beer in hand.

Corn Maze at Chatfield Farms - LINK
Friday through Sunday, September 25 to 27, Denver Botanic Gardens Chatfield Farms, 22 dollars adult.
Get lost in acres of stalks with a new design cut each year, plus farm animals and a working homestead nearby. A proper fall tradition for locals who want their pumpkin patch fix without the mall parking lot version.

The Denver Oktoberfest, 56th Annual - LINK
Friday through Sunday, September 25 to 27, Larimer Street and 21st, Ballpark District, Free general admission.
Stein hoisting, keg bowling, polka, a silent disco, and the Long Dog Derby, a full on dachshund race down Larimer. It is the second and final weekend of one of Denver's longest running street festivals, and the dog race alone is worth the trip.

Birding by Kayak - LINK
Sunday, September 27, Standley Lake, Arvada, Free with your own kayak, 10 dollar rental.
Paddle out at sunrise to spot shore and water birds from the water instead of the shoreline. It is a quietly odd, meditative way to birdwatch, and a nice excuse to get on the water before it gets cold.

Bright Nights at Four Mile - LINK
Wednesday through Sunday nightly, Four Mile Historic Park, from 15 dollars, 28 dollars adult.
A trail of oversized lantern creatures, giant insects, and African wildlife winds through Denver's oldest standing structure. Victorian storybook weird in a good way, and a lot more offbeat than your average light show.

Savor the Season - LINK
Tuesday, September 29 through Thursday, October 1, Truss House at RiNo ArtPark, 180 dollars per person.
Twenty four independent Denver chefs take over communal farm tables for a five course, family style dinner benefiting local nonprofits. It is EatDenver's biggest fall tradition and a genuine gathering of the city's indie restaurant scene, not a hotel ballroom gala.

Denver Volunteer Opportunity

We Don't Waste, Mobile Food Markets Volunteer - LINK
Volunteers help set up tents and tables, stock food, staff a station, or help shoppers use wagons, then break down free, twice weekly pop up grocery markets held at various Denver locations to fight food insecurity. Spanish speakers, even beginners, are especially needed to help with check in. We Don't Waste currently has 21 open shifts posted for individual volunteers, with each shift typically running two to three hours from setup through breakdown.
