Developing Denver

Colorado Breaks Ground on an 806 Million Dollar Highway Overhaul - LINK
Crews just broke ground on an 806 million dollar reconstruction of I-270 north of Denver, replacing 12 aging bridges and adding the corridor's first tolled express lanes, a six mile overhaul promising safer, faster trips between I-25 and I-70 through 2033.

Civic Center Park's 50 Million Dollar Facelift Breaks Ground - LINK
Denver Parks and Recreation is pouring up to 50 million dollars into reorienting the historic Greek Theatre, adding accessible pathways, and building a Gang of 19 disability rights memorial, with a grand reopening planned for 2028.

A 10 Million Dollar Disco Roller Rink Just Opened in Sun Valley - LINK
Rainbow Dome, a queer focused, disco themed skating venue born from a 2021 pop up series, opened its permanent 29,000 square foot home this week, giving Denver's arts and LGBTQ+ scene a brand new place to gather and glide.

Denver's Transit Riders Just Got a 50 Million Dollar Upgrade - LINK
The Federal Boulevard bus rapid transit project landed 50 million dollars from a state grant program, funding 74 upgraded stations with shelters, real time arrival displays, and lighting, with enhanced buses expected to start running the corridor by 2030.

Two International Grocery Chains Are Racing to Open in Denver - LINK
Japanese chain Hashi Market is moving into a former Aurora Safeway while Chinese chain One Plus One Supermarket plans its first United States store near Denver International Airport, a sign international grocers see real growth left in the metro.

Denver News

Denver's AI Rollout Is Way Ahead of Its Rulebook - LINK
A city auditor found Denver has no formal risk management policy for artificial intelligence and that the airport has no AI framework at all, even as departments adopt the tools faster than anyone is tracking what could go wrong.

Denver Zoo's Elephants Just Demolished a Pile of Giant Pumpkins - LINK
Keepers turned donated giant pumpkins into elephant enrichment at the annual Squish the Squash event, letting the herd stomp, toss, and snack their way through gourds sized for a problem solving, personality driven workout.

Aurora Could Freeze All New Data Centers Indefinitely - LINK
Aurora's city council is weighing an indefinite moratorium on new data center construction, a move that would put the brakes on one of the metro's fastest growing industries over concerns about power use and land.

An Anti Vax Dating Event Just Got Rejected by a Second Denver Business - LINK
The Golden Mill food hall turned away a 400 person singles meetup from unvaccinated dating app Unjected, which is already suing Recess Beer Garden over a canceled May event, before finding a new, amused host at the Grizzly Rose.

A Tiny Town's Traffic Gate Just Became National News - LINK
Bow Mar, a town of about 900 people, wants to gate its Sheridan Boulevard entrance to stop cut through traffic, and the fight with neighboring Denver and Littleton has gotten big enough that Denver is now considering its own gate in response.

Denver Is Finally Kicking Veo Off the Sidewalks - LINK
Starting November 1, the city will enforce its contract ban on sidewalk riding for Veo's electric bikes and scooters, setting up a clash over where riders can legally go after months of complaints about blocked walkways.

Lakewood Neighbors Call a Rezoning Request a Soup Sandwich - LINK
Layton Construction wants to rezone 11.4 acres on Yale Avenue for multifamily housing, and furious neighbors packed a hearing to call the plan a mess, though the planning commission unanimously recommended it move forward anyway.

Denver's Water Supply Just Dropped to Two Thirds Full - LINK
City reservoirs have fallen to 67 percent capacity, well below the usual 90 percent for this time of year, and officials warn levels could dip to 55 percent before spring runoff, a reality that already triggered Denver's first lawn watering ban in nearly 25 years.

Activists Are Quietly Counting Every ICE Flight Out of DIA - LINK
Three Colorado activists have been tracking deportation flights at Denver International Airport by counting detainees boarded onto planes, even after being pushed out of the terminal, and are now pressing for a live camera feed after a contested Key Lime Air lease vote.

Does Denver Have an Image Problem? One Urbanist Says No - LINK
A new Downtown Denver Partnership report from urbanist Richard Florida argues the city's 40 percent office vacancy rate is misleading and that Denver is already a thriving playground city, a claim local planning experts are pushing back on hard.

Denver City Council Members Call a Fentanyl Ballot Measure Egregious - LINK
Denver's Health and Safety Committee is urging voters to reject statewide Proposition 132, with council members calling the fentanyl measure egregious and arguing it would strain jails without actually reducing drug use across the city.

Weekly Denver Forecast

Denver eases into the week on the mild side, climbing from 63 on Monday to a sunny 69 on Tuesday, then holding steady in the upper 60s to low 70s through Friday with mostly clear skies. Overnight lows stay chilly in the upper 30s to mid 40s all week, so the jacket is not optional after dark. Saturday brings a slight chance of showers and a cooler 66, the only real wrinkle in an otherwise calm, sunny stretch.
