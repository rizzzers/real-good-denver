Weekly Denver Forecast

Denver's week starts on a sunny high note with Monday topping out near 80, but don't get used to it: a soggy pattern moves in Tuesday night and dumps on Wednesday, when a high of just 57 and an 80 percent shot at showers and thunderstorms will make you dig out a real coat. The sun returns by Thursday and sticks around through the weekend, climbing back into the low 70s by Saturday, so plan the patio hangs for after the midweek soak, not during it.
