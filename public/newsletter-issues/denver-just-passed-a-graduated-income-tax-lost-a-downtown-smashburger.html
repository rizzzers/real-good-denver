A Beam Signing for a Burned Down Bandstand - LINK
Denver Parks and Rec invited residents to sign a steel beam marking the rebuild of City Park's fire damaged bandstand, with the restored landmark on track to reopen for the 2027 City Park Jazz season.

Mini Golf Finds Its LoDo Home - LINK
California based Tipsy Putt is opening its first venue outside the state at 1755 Blake Street, bringing a 12,500 square foot indoor nine hole course, a full bar, and a DJ booth to Lower Downtown before year's end.

Rossonian Hotel Finally Gets Its Green Light - LINK
Denver approved the formal site plan for the historic Rossonian Hotel in Five Points, clearing the way for a 126 room hotel and restaurant anchored by the jazz landmark that once hosted Duke Ellington and Billie Holiday.

A Food Hall Lines Up Next to the Future Broncos Site - LINK
Thrive Development unveiled updated plans for Lincoln Station, a food hall, beer garden, and coworking complex just north of Burnham Yard, the site the Broncos are eyeing for a future stadium.

Four Seasons Brings Standalone Residences to Cherry Creek - LINK
An eight story, 42 unit Four Seasons branded residence is moving forward in Cherry Creek, Colorado's first standalone Four Seasons property, with units ranging from one bedroom layouts to 6,000 square foot spreads.

Denver News

Lakewood Loses an Officer to a Bus Shootout - LINK
A Lakewood police officer was killed and three others wounded after a man opened fire aboard an RTD bus near Colfax and Wadsworth, a tragedy that has shaken commuters across the metro all week.

Why Lakewood Police Are Called Agents, Not Officers - LINK
Amid the grief over this week's shooting, a lot of Denver area readers are learning for the first time that Lakewood's police force has used the title agent instead of officer since its founding, a quirk of local history suddenly back in the spotlight.

Blucifer Gets a Glow Up - LINK
DIA's infamous 32 foot blue mustang, the fiberglass sculpture famous for its glowing red eyes and the accident that killed its own creator, is getting a maintenance touch up, giving Denver's most notorious airport landmark a fresh coat before its close up.

Jefferson County's School Tax Comes Down to Five Votes - LINK
A 73 million dollar Jeffco Public Schools property tax measure is headed to a recount after just five votes separated passing from failing, with a loss threatening at least 13 million dollars in cuts to teacher pay and retention.

A Denver Nonprofit Doubles Down on Second Chances - LINK
Team HWH moved into a Swansea facility twice the size of its old kitchen, using a culinary training program and a guaranteed jobs partnership with Brookdale Senior Living to help up to 200 people a year facing housing instability find steady work.

Champagne Cascade Sets a Fundraising Pace at the Brown Palace - LINK
The Brown Palace's 38th annual Champagne Cascade poured over a pyramid of more than 6,000 glasses to kick off the holiday season, with a 20,000 dollar winning bid pushing this year's Denver Rescue Mission fundraising total toward a new record.

Police Make an Arrest in a Fatal Halloween Shooting - LINK
Denver police arrested 23 year old Deontre Yorks in Colorado Springs after tracing shell casings, surveillance footage, and a telltale Halloween mask back to an October 31 shooting that killed a 46 year old man on East 40th Avenue.

Jennifer Bacon Becomes Colorado's Next House Speaker - LINK
Denver Representative Jennifer Bacon was chosen as the next Colorado House Speaker, becoming the first Black woman to hold the job as Denver lawmakers also lock down the Senate presidency and the House majority leader post.

Colorado Voters Scrap the Flat Income Tax - LINK
Colorado voters narrowly approved a graduated income tax to replace the state's decades old flat rate, a change expected to raise 2.7 billion dollars while cutting taxes for anyone earning under 500,000 dollars a year.

A Downtown Steakhouse Replaces an Old Denver Landmark - LINK
Tavern on Blake Street owner Jeff Moerke is opening Nine9Nine Steakhouse, Seafood and Raw Bar in the former 801 Fish space downtown, aiming for a grand opening before the end of the year.

A Real Estate Backed Council Candidate Calls It Quits - LINK
Monica Martinez, whose city council bid drew real estate industry backing, says she will not run again after finishing second to Melissa Mejia, choosing instead to refocus on her nonprofit work revitalizing East Colfax.

Weekly Denver Forecast

Denver settles into a genuinely gorgeous stretch this week, with mostly clear skies and highs climbing from the upper 50s Tuesday into the mid 60s by Friday before a slightly cooler, partly cloudy weekend. Overnight lows stay comfortably in the low to mid 30s all week with no precipitation in sight, so hang onto those patio plans a little longer. Veterans Day Wednesday looks like one of the nicer days of the bunch, proof November can still throw Denver a bone before winter actually shows up.

Denver Volunteer Opportunity

Food Bank of the Rockies, Denver Distribution Center Volunteer - LINK
With Colorado hunger relief groups bracing for a harder winter and the Food Bank of the Rockies running six days a week to keep up, volunteers are needed at the Denver distribution center to sort, repack, and prep food for the hundreds of hunger relief programs it supplies statewide. Morning shifts start at 8:30 or 8:45am and afternoon shifts start at 1pm, both lasting a few hours, with no experience required and group shifts available for families, coworkers, or friends who want to show up together.
