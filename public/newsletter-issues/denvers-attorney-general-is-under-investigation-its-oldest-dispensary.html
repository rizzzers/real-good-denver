Cool stuff happening at Red Rocks, courtesy of Doug, who sent us this one.

Doug was up there hitting the stairs when he noticed a crew working on the old juniper trees lining the amphitheater.

So naturally, he asked questions.

Turns out, many of these Rocky Mountain junipers have been there since Red Rocks was built. They were transplanted from elsewhere in the park and have spent the better part of 85 years surviving concerts, Colorado weather and millions of people stomping around on their root systems.

Now they’re getting some love.

Crews are aerating the heavily compacted soil and injecting water, nutrients and mycorrhizal fungi around the roots to help stimulate new growth.

Basically, Red Rocks is giving its trees a spa day.

And considering the alternative is eventually replacing them, we’re all for it.

So next time you’re up there, give one a hug.

Just maybe don’t stand on its roots while you do it.

Thanks for sending this one in, Doug.
