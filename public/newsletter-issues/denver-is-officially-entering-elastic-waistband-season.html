Developing Denver

Denver Could Make Construction Relief for Small Businesses Permanent - LINK
Denver City Council is weighing a plan to make the Business Impact Opportunity Fund permanent, giving struggling Colfax Avenue merchants a reliable safety net after it has already helped more than 3,000 small businesses hurt by city construction since 2019.

A Texas Tea Chain Picks Green Valley Ranch for Its Denver Debut - LINK
HTeaO submitted concept plans for a drive-thru location at Tower Road and Green Valley Ranch Boulevard, marking the Texas iced tea chain's first shot at Denver proper after opening its first Colorado store in Loveland last year.

A Fire-Gutted West Colfax Building Could Become 64 Affordable Homes - LINK
Urban Land Conservancy's plan for the fire-damaged Liberty House Apartments calls for 64 income-restricted units on a 99-year affordable land trust, giving a vacant Hooker Street eyesore a real second life.

Parisi Is Rebuilding on Tennyson Street After Its May Fire - LINK
The beloved Berkeley neighborhood Italian spot cleared its concept plan review and is aiming for a November 2027 reopening, bringing updated dining rooms, new storefront windows, and a steel corner awning back to 44th and Tennyson.

A Denver Apartment Complex Just Landed a 62.5 Million Dollar Refinancing - LINK
The Bascom Group closed a 62.5 million dollar refinancing on Viceroy Observatory Park, a sign investors still see real money in Denver's apartment stock even with the market sluggish elsewhere.

Denver News

Denver and Littleton Are Suing a Tiny Suburb Over Traffic Gates - LINK
Denver and Littleton sued the small town of Bow Mar to block its plan to gate two roadways and close a third to outsiders, arguing the move would dump traffic onto neighboring streets and slow emergency response.

A Centennial Rocket Company Lost 40 Amazon Satellites Over the Weekend - LINK
A Vulcan rocket from Centennial-based United Launch Alliance suffered an anomaly about 10 minutes after launch and failed to deliver its Amazon mission to orbit, taking 40 satellites down with it.

Denver Just Flipped One of the Safest Republican Seats in the Country - LINK
Democrat Eileen Laubacher unseated Lauren Boebert in Colorado's 4th Congressional District by less than one percentage point, an upset in a seat Trump carried by 18 points two years ago that leaves Colorado with a single House Republican.

Progressive Protesters Crashed Hakeem Jeffries' Own Denver Conference - LINK
House Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries got interrupted by his own party's progressive wing during a post-midterms gathering in Denver, a sign the celebration mood didn't last long.

A Shark Tank Pickleball Company Is Weighing Whether to Leave Colorado - LINK
Pepper Pong, the ping-pong and pickleball hybrid that landed a 150,000 dollar Shark Tank deal, is tracking toward 6 million dollars in sales this year but is now considering a move out of state after Amendment 87 pushed Colorado's top income tax rate to 8.4 percent.

A LoDo 7-Eleven Is Closing After a Running Fight Over Vagrancy - LINK
The 18th and Blake 7-Eleven is shutting down by November 30 after becoming a regular gathering spot for transient people, with a worker blaming theft and corporate citing an effort to optimize its portfolio.

Denver's Foie Gras Ban Just Kicked In, and Restaurants Are Already Adjusting - LINK
Weeks after voters approved Initiated Ordinance 311 banning the sale of force-fed duck liver, Denver restaurants are reworking menus and sourcing to comply with the city's new foie gras ban.

Denver's School Board Voted to Close a Tiny HBCU-Style High School - LINK
Board members approved a split-vote closure of Robert F. Smith STEAM Academy after enrollment dropped to just 24 students, ending a six-year run for the far northeast Denver school modeled on historically Black colleges.

Barry Morphew Pleads Guilty in a Denver Hit-and-Run, Avoiding a Bigger Mess - LINK
Morphew, who still faces a murder charge in his wife Suzanne's death, pleaded guilty to careless driving in a separate Denver hit-and-run case and got 60 days in jail in exchange for four other charges being dropped.

A CU Buff Just Made History Leading Ralphie Onto the Field - LINK
Senior Emma Morgan became the first woman to lead Ralphie the Buffalo from the front position onto Folsom Field, breaking a barrier that had stood since the tradition began.

A Lakewood Couple Allegedly Hid a Body Under an Air Mattress for 18 Months - LINK
Suzanne and James Agnew are accused of keeping their lover Jim O'Neill's decomposed body hidden beside their bed for a year and a half while cashing his Social Security checks, years after a 1995 "wedding" at his father's gravesite.

Weekly Denver Forecast

Denver is holding onto a genuinely dry, mild stretch heading into the holiday run, with highs sliding gently from the mid 60s Tuesday into the 50s by the weekend and overnight lows dipping into the 20s and 30s. The only blemish is a slight chance of rain and snow showers Friday night into Saturday, otherwise expect mostly sunny skies straight through. Pack a light jacket, not a parka, and maybe hold off on the real coat until Zoo Lights season actually feels like it.
