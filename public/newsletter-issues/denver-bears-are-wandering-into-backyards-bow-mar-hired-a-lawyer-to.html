Denver News

Denver bears are wandering into Harvey Park backyards - LINK
A mother bear and her cub have been spotted padding through Harvey Park and Marston toward the Platte River, part of a drought driven surge that has already brought Colorado Parks and Wildlife nearly 6,129 bear reports this year, almost double last year's count.

Bow Mar just hired a lawyer and a PR firm to defend its plan to gate off public streets - LINK
The tiny 900 person suburb wants RFID gated entry on public roads, and Denver's mayor is so unhappy about it he is threatening to build his own barricades to trap Bow Mar residents inside.

Denver installed a street sign that misspells the state it lives in - LINK
A downtown sign reading Califorina instead of California went up Monday, got mocked into a Red Hot Chili Peppers meme by Tuesday morning, and was yanked the same day, with the contractor eating the cost of the redo.

White House emails reportedly show a 3 a.m. plot to punish Colorado over Tina Peters - LINK
A leaked email allegedly links a Trump administration official's late night message about Colorado to the eventual closure of a Boulder science center and 615 million dollars in energy funding cuts, and the state's attorney general is calling it a smoking gun.

Denver Water is bankrolling a ballot measure to fight the next Hayman scale wildfire - LINK
Proposition 308 would redirect state sales tax surplus money, roughly 175 million dollars a year, toward wildfire prevention and watershed protection after four of Colorado's largest fires have ignited in just the last six years.

Colorado just got soaked and the drought barely noticed - LINK
Heavy rain flooded roads and trails across the Front Range this week, but water officials say the storms were nowhere near enough to pull the state out of a drought that keeps getting worse.

A Denver Gazette columnist says it's time for the Broncos to ditch their own stadium site - LINK
Woody Paige argues Burnham Yard's neighborhood fights and Denver Water relocation headaches make Lone Tree, with its highway access and reserved 10 million square feet, the smarter bet for the Broncos' next home.

Denver Botanic Gardens is opening an 18th wedding venue because everyone wants to get married in a glasshouse - LINK
The new Ponderosa Glasshouse seats about 50 guests among the pines, and it's arriving because the gardens now field more than 220 wedding requests a year, with some venues already booked into 2028.

Colorado's drought has neighbors snitching on neighbors over sprinklers - LINK
Denver Water has logged about 5,500 watering complaints since launching a tip line this summer, and while Aurora blew past its conservation goal, Denver is still lagging far behind its own target.

A Golden fire chief and city manager both got fired over a doll hung by its neck in a fire station - LINK
Newly released records show a firefighter admitted hanging a Black novelty doll from a rope in March, and the fallout has already cost the city manager and fire chief their jobs, with the chief now threatening to sue.

Denver's biggest firefighters union is suing over a 389,000 dollar bill for dues it says it doesn't owe - LINK
Local 858 voted to leave the statewide firefighters union after members said they weren't getting their money's worth, and now the state group wants nearly 389,000 dollars in back dues the local says it never agreed to pay.

Denver Volunteer Opportunity

Food Bank of the Rockies, Warehouse Volunteer - LINK 
Volunteers sort, pack, and prepare food donations for distribution to Denver area families at the food bank's Main Distribution Center. Colorado hunger has climbed to a 10 year high after federal SNAP and TEFAP cuts shrank the food bank's supply, and this summer's heat waves and lingering wildfire smoke across the Front Range have made things even tighter for families getting by. Shifts run about three hours, Monday through Saturday, mornings or afternoons, no experience required to sign up.
