Denver Spent $1.1 Million Figuring Out Exactly Where Your Park Porta Potty Goes - LINK
A public records request uncovered the actual spreadsheet Denver Parks and Rec uses to score every park on attendance, equity, and nearby bathrooms before deciding how many portable toilets it deserves, totaling 251 units across the city each season.

Denver Spent Almost $1 Million on New Pickleball Courts, Then Banned Pickleball From Them - LINK
Skyland Park's freshly resurfaced courts are tennis only now, so pickleball players have to trek a mile and a half to Martin Luther King Jr. Park under new rules requiring courts to sit 350 feet from homes after noise complaints.

Denver's Robotaxis Are Getting Pelted With Eggs - LINK
Riders say Waymo's driverless cars have been egged at least twice since launching in September, including a windshield hit the car did not even seem to register, and nobody yet knows who is behind it.

The Bag Lady of Castle Rock Has an Active Warrant and Somehow Still Is Not Stopping - LINK
Katherine Brown has racked up trespassing citations and an outstanding warrant for allegedly pressuring strangers for rides and cash, yet residents say she keeps finding new drivers to target across the south metro.

A $170 Million Downtown Tower Just Got Seized After Its Owners No Showed Court - LINK
The 455 unit X Denver tower is now under receivership after its developer defaulted on a massive loan and skipped the hearing entirely, the latest sign of just how shaky downtown's commercial real estate market has become.

A Denver Protester Is Suing a Police Sergeant by Name Over a Tackle at Last Year's No Kings Rally - LINK

Denver keeps riding an unseasonably warm stretch this week, climbing from the low 70s into the upper 70s by Thursday and Friday before a slight chance of showers finally shows up Thursday night and lingers into the weekend.
